# v2.0.0-preview1

> **Preview Release** · November 25, 2026 · [NuGet](https://www.nuget.org/packages/WinUI.TableView/2.0.0-preview1) · [GitHub Release](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/releases/tag/v2.0.0-preview1)

This is the first preview of the `WinUI.TableView` v2.0.0. `TableView` no longer derives from `ListView`: it is a `Control` that hosts its rows in an `ItemsRepeater` and creates, recycles and positions them itself. This rewrite makes grouping available on every Uno Platform target, stores selections as ranges, and adds column virtualization, so tables with very large row and column counts load and scroll quickly. This preview also adds row numbers, Korean localization, better touch support on WinUI and Uno Platform, and a redesigned sample application.

## ⚠️ Breaking Changes

Most XAML and C# code keeps compiling: the members most apps use, such as `ItemsSource`, `SelectedItem`, `SelectionMode` and `ScrollIntoView`, are declared on `TableView` with the same names and types. The main changes are:

- `TableView` derives from `Control` instead of `ListView`, and `TableViewRow` from `ContentControl` instead of `ListViewItem`. Members inherited from `ListView`, such as `ItemTemplate`, `ItemContainerStyle` and `ItemClick`, are removed, and so is row drag-reorder ([#499](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/499)).
- Custom row, group header and cell templates may need changes ([#499](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/499)).
- Sorting, grouping or expanding and collapsing a group clears the selection ([#499](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/499)).
- With column virtualization, `Cells`, `Headers` and `HeaderControl` only cover columns in or near the viewport ([#505](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/505)).
- One click or drag raises a single `SelectionChanged` or `CellSelectionChanged` event ([#508](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/508)).

📖 Read **[Breaking changes in v2.0](../docs/breaking-changes-v2.md)** for the full list and what to do for each change.

## 🌟 Features

- Rows are now hosted in an `ItemsRepeater` with the platform's virtualizing `StackLayout`. Rows are pooled and reused, row and cell selections are stored as index ranges, and the default cell template is reduced to the elements every cell needs by [@w-ahmad](https://github.com/w-ahmad) in [#499](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/499).
- Grouping now works on every Uno Platform target. Group headers are virtualized like any other row, and the sticky group header is drawn over the rows by [@w-ahmad](https://github.com/w-ahmad) in [#499](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/499).
- Added column virtualization. Rows and the header row only create cells and headers for frozen columns and for scrollable columns in or near the viewport, so a table with hundreds of columns loads and scrolls like a narrow one. Adding 1,000 columns went from about 5 s to under 0.5 s by [@w-ahmad](https://github.com/w-ahmad) in [#505](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/505).
- Added row numbers with the new `ShowRowNumbers` property, along with `RowNumberColumnWidth` and `TableViewRow.RowNumber`. Row numbers can be styled with the `TableViewRowNumberFontSize`, `TableViewRowNumberForeground`, `TableViewRowNumberMargin` and `TableViewRowNumberMinWidth` theme resources by [@w-ahmad](https://github.com/w-ahmad) in [#426](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/426).
- Added Korean (`ko-KR`) localization by [@luckypong](https://github.com/luckypong) in [#477](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/477).

## Enhancements

- Removed `GeneratedBindableCustomProperty` from all library types. Row heights, header content, Date/Time formats and ComboBox editor settings are now set directly instead of through bindings, and `TableViewGroupInfo` gains `KeyProperty` and `CountProperty`. This keeps the library working under Native AOT, including columns created in code by [@w-ahmad](https://github.com/w-ahmad) in [#445](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/445).
- Improved touch support. On WinUI and Uno Platform, rows and cells no longer show the pointer-over look for touch, and a column can be resized by dragging its header edge with a finger. On Uno Platform, a double tap on a cell now starts editing and raises `CellDoubleTapped` and `RowDoubleTapped` by [@w-ahmad](https://github.com/w-ahmad) in [#507](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/507).
- Added horizontal touch scrolling on Uno Platform. Rows, headers, frozen columns and the scrollbar now move together, the horizontal scrollbar shows without a vertical scrollbar and hides itself after a few seconds, and a tap selects rows or cells instead of starting a drag selection by [@luckypong](https://github.com/luckypong) in [#502](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/502).
- Cells outside the viewport are left out of layout and rendering on Uno Platform Skia targets by [@w-ahmad](https://github.com/w-ahmad) in [#499](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/499).
- The table, row and group header automation peers implement their patterns themselves, and `IGridProvider.GetItem` now realizes rows and cells that are out of view by [@w-ahmad](https://github.com/w-ahmad) in [#499](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/499) and [#505](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/505).

## 🐛 Fixes

- Fixed <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>A</kbd> doing nothing with `SelectionUnit="Cell"` and a multi-select mode. It now selects every cell by [@w-ahmad](https://github.com/w-ahmad) in [#499](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/499).
- Fixed an empty incrementally loading source not being asked for its first page in some cases by [@w-ahmad](https://github.com/w-ahmad) in [#499](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/499).
- Fixed the table staying in drag selection, with the row looking pressed, after a lost pointer capture such as an alt-tab or a cancelled touch by [@w-ahmad](https://github.com/w-ahmad) in [#499](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/499).
- Fixed an edit being dropped when its row was recycled, which left `IsEditing` set and disabled the corner menu. The edit is now committed when the row is released by [@w-ahmad](https://github.com/w-ahmad) in [#499](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/499).
- Fixed row selection through UI Automation ignoring `SelectionUnit` and the selection anchor by [@w-ahmad](https://github.com/w-ahmad) in [#499](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/499).
- Fixed a column that is added to `Columns` twice losing its header, Date/Time format and `ClockIdentifier` updates when one of its entries is removed by [@w-ahmad](https://github.com/w-ahmad) in [#445](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/445).
- Fixed row headers not growing back when a row header template is set again after being removed, and the cells jumping for one frame when a template is set by [@w-ahmad](https://github.com/w-ahmad) in [#426](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/426).
- Fixed headers drifting out of alignment with the cells when a column was resized after a horizontal scroll on Uno Platform by [@luckypong](https://github.com/luckypong) in [#502](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/502).
- Fixed a star (`*`) column with a `MaxWidth` collapsing to its minimum width when no other star column was left to share the space by [@w-ahmad](https://github.com/w-ahmad) in [#514](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/514).
- Fixed the cell a drag selection started on keeping its highlighted look after the pointer was released on Uno Platform by [@w-ahmad](https://github.com/w-ahmad) in [#509](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/509).
- Fixed the row details panel moving to the right, often out of view, when its row was laid out again. On a table without visible row headers, the details now start where the cells start by [@w-ahmad](https://github.com/w-ahmad) in [#510](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/510).
- Fixed row numbers, row headers and the details button pushing the cells to the right under nested grouping instead of filling the group indent first by [@w-ahmad](https://github.com/w-ahmad) in [#513](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/513).
- Fixed group headers swapping their pointer-over and focus look between groups when a group was expanded or collapsed by [@w-ahmad](https://github.com/w-ahmad) in [#511](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/511).
- Fixed a group header's count being pushed out of view when the columns were wider than the table. Group headers are now laid out in the viewport's width by [@w-ahmad](https://github.com/w-ahmad) in [#512](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/512).
- Fixed a single click raising `SelectionChanged` or `CellSelectionChanged` twice, once to clear the old selection and once to add the new one by [@w-ahmad](https://github.com/w-ahmad) in [#508](https://github.com/w-ahmad/WinUI.TableView/pull/508).
